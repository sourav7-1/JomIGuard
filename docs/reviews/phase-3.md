## 3. For an expert: limits and things to verify

From `docs/specs/rules.md` (Known limits) and `docs/legal-notes.md` (Actions to verify):

1. **Owner area in a plot = share_fraction × plot area** (Decision 3). Needed by
   `sold_within_share` and `co_owner_consent`.
2. **Heir shares come from the mutation / BS khatian**; no inheritance-share calculation
   (Decision 5).
3. **dag numbers can change between surveys**; `plots_on_khatian` compares with the current
   khatian only.
4. **Owners for seller checks:** rules use the current khatian; `findings.md` says "mutation if
   present". Which record is authoritative when they differ?
5. **Consent:** only co-sellers on the same deed count; power of attorney (আমমোক্তারনামা),
   separate consent documents or court orders are not recognised. What form must co-heirs'
   consent take?
6. **Chain order** uses survey order and record/registration dates when present; documents
   without dates are assumed to follow survey order.
7. **Units:** only একর / শতাংশ / decimal are converted; বিঘা, কাঠা, আনা are reported, never
   converted. Area of an owner whose plot is shared with another khatian.
8. **Same name, same father:** two owners cannot be told apart; nicknames, initials and
   transliteration are not matched.
9. **Actions shown to users:** field survey by an amin; mortgage and court-case search;
   co-heirs' written consent or power of attorney; certified khatian copy; asking the land
   office about a missing heir or wrong share sum (objection / correction case?); tracing a
   broken chain with a lawyer.
10. **Identity verification** (`docs/specs/identity.md`): data protection law, e-KYC rules,
    consent wording, retention period.
