Shortcuts
  • Click add point · click the first point or Enter to close
  • ⌫ remove the last point · Esc cancel the unfinished drawing
  • 1–0 pick the class for new polygons
  • D points · B freehand (press, hold, draw, release) · S select/move points · E erase (hover = red, click = delete)
  • Tablet: draw with pen or finger · two fingers to pinch-zoom and move

  • Ctrl+Z undo · Ctrl+Y redo
